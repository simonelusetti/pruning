1. WHAT A conv1 CHANNEL IS
==============================================================================
channel i of conv1 = one 3x3 filter W1[i] (+ bias b1[i]) slid over the image -> one 8x8 map a_i(x)
conv2 reads channel i through the slices W2[:, i] (a 3x3 kernel for each of its 32 filters)
removing channel i frees fan-in 10 + fan-out 288 = 298 parameters
